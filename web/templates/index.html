How the number is built

Three channels each output a probability of genuine. They are combined in logit space (log-odds), with learned weights and a bias, then mapped back to a probability. Open Model channels for weights and per-channel scores.

Heuristic checks

Rule-based risk in [0,1] (higher = more suspicious). Combined into one signal for the ensemble.

Tabular model — top contributions

Logistic regression on scaled features. Δ logit = coefficient × scaled value; larger magnitude = stronger push toward genuine (+) or deceptive (−).
